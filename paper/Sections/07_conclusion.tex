\section{Conclusion}

PCU-Select treats data utility as specific to the target PEFT and task while reusing expensive supervision across configurations in a registry. It maps different PEFT families to 24 shared intervention sites, allowing one conditional scorer to produce a distinct subset for each target without rebuilding a gradient datastore (\S\ref{sec:method}). Across four tasks and five seen configurations, PCU-Select averages 35.18 versus 35.14 for per-PEFT LESS and reduces the marginal cost of adding one four-task PEFT from 63.2 to 1.51 GPU-hours, a $42\times$ reduction (Tables~\ref{tab:main-results} and~\ref{tab:quality-cost-headline}). Transfer follows structural support: near-support configurations use zero-shot scoring, while reduced calibration closes most of the gap for farther configurations and LN Tuning. PCU-Select therefore preserves target-specific selection while replacing per-configuration datastore construction with reusable offline computation.
