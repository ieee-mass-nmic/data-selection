Supplementary Analyses
Component ablations. Table S9 reports the full compo-
nent breakdown summarized in the main analysis. Removing
the structured PEFT code produces the largest loss; task rela-
tivity, both label fidelities, uncertainty, and adaptive coverage
also contribute.
Cross-PEFT transfer. Table S10 supports the introduc-
tion’s 2.0-point cross-family mismatch penalty. Performance
falls as the source PEFT moves farther from the target PEFT.
Figure S1 shows the same mechanism from the selection
side—PCU-Select’s subset overlap falls as configurations di-
verge, while PEFT-agnostic RDS+ remains exactly 1.000.
Unseen-configuration transfer. Table S11 is the paired
source for Figure 3 in the main paper. Near-support targets
transfer zero-shot. Calibration closes most of the gap for far
same-family targets and LN Tuning. For Prefix/P-Tuning, the
point estimate falls from 6.97 to 1.99, leaving a stronger
transfer boundary. The 0.18 cal-200-to-cal-500 change is de-
scriptive rather than evidence of a reliable difference between
the two label budgets.
